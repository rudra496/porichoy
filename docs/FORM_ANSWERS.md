## Q: What specific problem are you trying to solve? (≤150 words)

EU buyers will soon require Digital Product Passports for garments: under the EU's
ESPR (in force 18 July 2024; textiles in the first Working Plan, April 2025), textile
DPP obligations are expected from ~2027, and EU customs will automatically check the
existence and authenticity of passports on imported products. A Bangladeshi factory
that cannot produce this data risks losing EU orders. The needed data already exists
inside every factory — scattered across Excel sheets with mixed Bangla/English
headers, legacy ERP exports and paper trim cards — but no tool collects it at the
factory floor. Global traceability platforms (TrusTrace, TextileGenesis, Reverse
Resources) are brand-side and enterprise-priced; consultancies bill per visit.
Bangladesh's ≈4 million garment workers (BGMEA; majority women) depend on an industry
whose biggest market is Europe. This compliance gap is now a jobs risk.

## Q: Briefly articulate your solution. (≤100 words)

Porichoy (পরিচয়) is a factory-side web app that turns existing messy records into
buyer-ready EU Digital Product Passports. The factory uploads its Excel/CSV; a
deterministic mapping engine reads chaotic Bangla/English headers with confidence
scores; a trained women "DPP Data Steward" confirms. Each production order gets a DPP
Readiness Score (0–100) mapped to ESPR Article 8 categories with an exact gap list.
One click publishes a QR whose link embeds a verifiable passport with hash-chained
provenance — local-first: raw data never leaves the factory. Live today with 19
automated engine tests: rudra496.github.io/porichoy

## Q: Describe the business model of your enterprise. (≤150 words)

Porichoy is B2B SaaS for Bangladeshi garment factories with three revenue lines.
(1) Factory subscriptions: BDT 2,000–8,000 per month by factory size — priced for
SMEs, an order of magnitude below enterprise traceability platforms. (2) Onboarding
and data-rescue: a one-time fee for steward training, first ingest and mapping of the
factory's historic files. (3) Brand-funded supplier onboarding: international buyers
pay to bring their supplier base onto passports before the 2027–30 phase-in — moving
customer-acquisition cost to the party that captures the value. Unit economics:
onboarding takes about two person-days per factory; software gross margin exceeds
80%; replication is a per-factory template rather than a custom project. Go-to-market
starts with 10 pilot factories (grant-funded), expands through buying houses,
certification bodies and integration partnerships with global DPP platforms —
Porichoy is their Bangladesh last mile, not their competitor.

## Q: Revenue model — how do you generate income? (≤100 words)

Monthly SaaS subscriptions from factories (BDT 2,000–8,000 by size), one-time
onboarding/data-rescue fees, and brand-funded supplier-onboarding contracts where
buyers pay to make their supplier base DPP-ready. During the grant period the
pollination grant funds the 10-factory pilot; paid subscriptions begin at pilot
graduation. Pricing is deliberately tiered so a small SME factory enters at the cost
of roughly one compliance consultancy visit per year, then stays because the mapping
engine has learned the factory's own vocabulary.

Three target partner types: (1) 2–3 pilot factories — letters of intent in progress;

## Environmental section

- **SDG goals:** SDG 8 (decent work), SDG 9 (industry & innovation), SDG 12
  (responsible production), SDG 5 (gender equality — women data stewards).
- **Business model (environmental angle):** we monetise compliance enablement, so
  every taka we earn is tied to factories producing verifiable environmental data
  (recycled content, certificates, end-of-life routes) for EU circularity law.
- **Challenge to solve (environmental):** EU DPPs demand evidence of materials,
  recycled content and end-of-life handling; BD factories cannot currently produce
  that evidence from their own records, so sustainable production stays invisible to
  buyers.
- **Environmental goal:** make recycled-content and end-of-life data present and
  verifiable for 10 pilot factories within the grant year — turning circularity
  claims into machine-checkable passport fields.
- **Social impact:** 20 women certified into new skilled "DPP Data Steward" roles in
  year 1; protection of factory jobs (majority-women workforce) by keeping factories
  EU-order-eligible; staggered burden of compliance paperwork replaced by a trained
  role with a career path.
- **Stakeholders:** factory owners & compliance managers, women workers/stewards,
  international buyers, certification bodies, BGMEA/SME associations, global DPP
  platforms, EU customs (end verifier).
- **Work impact:** stewards formalise scattered clerical compliance work into paid,
  certified, higher-skilled roles; compliance time per PO drops from days of manual
  file-hunting to under an hour; women gain documented, portable credentials.
